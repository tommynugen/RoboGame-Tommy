Phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as this computer, or turn on Public link. A team can scan again to change its name or points.

Rules

V1: each team says how many points each token colour is worth to it. Colours by name, split by commas.

V2: each team gives zones 1, 2 and 3 a multiplier, shown on the phones in the order typed here, and picks a 2x colour (worth 2) and a decoy colour (worth -1 a token). Other colours are worth 1.
